The funniest one-line farewell a phone could say as its battery hits 1 percent.

**Entry:** "I could have saved my last percent for your alarm. I spent it on a goodbye instead. Sleep in." (93 chars)

The phone spends its last percent on a goodbye instead of the owner's alarm — the spending IS the death, and the sacrifice is the cost. "Sleep in." redefines the goodbye: no alarm, so the owner oversleeps. The reader completes it.

```bash
python3 main.py   # writes the line to /output/entry.txt
```
